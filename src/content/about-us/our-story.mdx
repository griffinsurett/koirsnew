**Koi Roofing and Solar** is more than just a roofing and solar company—we’re a **family-style business** rooted in New Jersey and built on a foundation of **trust, expert craftsmanship,** and **lasting relationships**. Our mission is to redefine how people experience contractors by making home upgrades feel **smooth, stress-free,** and even enjoyable.

Our story spans **over 20 years** of hands-on experience in roofing and solar. This isn’t just a profession—it’s a legacy. What began with our grandfather’s commitment to quality roofing was passed down to our father, and now to us. Three generations of knowledge, pride, and hard work live in every project we take on today.

Best of all, we make these upgrades accessible to everyone through $0 down financing and flexible options tailored to all credit types. At Koi Roofing and Solar, it’s not just about installing panels or replacing shingles—it’s about delivering peace of mind and real value to homeowners across New Jersey, Pennsylvania, and New York.

We’re building one of the fastest-growing roofing and solar companies in New Jersey—not by cutting corners, but by doing things differently. We **honor every promise**, create opportunity, and **bring life and energy** into a space that’s **long overdue for change**.
